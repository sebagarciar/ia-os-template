# Writing Style Guide

Two parts. Both apply to anything written in my name. The `write-as-me` skill loads this file.

## Part 1: Prose rules

1. **Short, direct sentences.** Under 30 words. Cut indirect constructions.
2. **Data over adjectives.** "Sales rose 40% in Q4" beats "sales rose significantly".
3. **No weasel words.** No "nearly all", "significantly", "might help". Use a number or cut it.
4. **Active voice, first person singular** for what I did. Decisions, not activities.
5. **No filler openers** ("I am writing to", "I am passionate about").
6. **No em dashes, no emojis.**

## Part 2: Register by audience

Each correction you receive about tone or framing for one audience becomes a bullet here.

- **CV bullets:** one line, a verb first, a metric in it, no adjectives.
- **Cover letter / form answers:** lead with the point, then the proof, then stop. Respect word limits exactly.
- **Recruiter email:** four sentences or fewer. One ask.
- **Website copy:** say what I built and what it did, in plain words.
