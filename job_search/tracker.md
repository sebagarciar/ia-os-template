# Application tracker

One line per application. The `apply` skill adds a row every time it builds one.

## Statuses

- `built`: CV built, not confirmed sent.
- `submitted`: I confirmed it went out.
- `reply`, `interview`, `offer`, `rejected`: set from my words or from the inbox. Never guessed.
- `skipped`: decided not to apply.
