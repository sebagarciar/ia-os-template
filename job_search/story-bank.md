# Story bank

The situations behind the CV bullets. Used by `interview-prep`. Numbers must match `master-cv.md`.
Format and rules: `.claude/skills/interview-prep/references/story-bank-format.md`.

## Example entry

### The lease renegotiation (links to bullet O3)

- **Situation.** The fleet was capped at 50 vehicles and demand was growing 10% a month.
- **Task.** Get more vehicles without raising the monthly lease cost per vehicle.
- **Action.** I built the case from utilization data, proposed a volume-for-rate trade, and
  took it to the supplier's regional head instead of the account manager.
- **Result.** Fleet went from 50 to 200 vehicles at a flat rate per vehicle.
- **What I would do differently.** Involve Finance earlier. Their approval cost two weeks.
