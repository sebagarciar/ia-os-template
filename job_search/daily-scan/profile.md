# Job scan profile

The brief for `/find-jobs`. The script reads two sections: **Search terms** and **Title
exclusions**. Claude reads all of it to rank. Facts about me come from `master-cv.md`.

## Location: hard rule

- Springfield on-site or hybrid. Edit the city lists at the top of
  `.claude/skills/find-jobs/scripts/scan.py` and the city strings in `sources.py` to match.
- Fully remote, open to people in my country.

## Target role families

1. Strategy and operations, business operations
2. AI adoption and transformation
3. Product operations, chief of staff

## What makes a job a 90

- The role mixes strategy and execution: own a metric, a budget or a launch.
- AI is at the core of the role, not a buzzword in the ad.

## What pulls the score down

- Below my level, or sales quota roles, or pure engineering.
- A must-have with no evidence in `master-cv.md` (say it as a gap, do not hide the job).

## Search terms

- operations manager
- strategy and operations
- ai adoption
- chief of staff

## Title exclusions

- intern
- account executive
- software engineer
